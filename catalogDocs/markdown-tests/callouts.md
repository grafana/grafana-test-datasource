## Note

> [!NOTE]
> A note with **bold** text and a [link](./links.md).

## Tip

> [!TIP]
> A tip that helps the reader.

## Important

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Information the reader needs to succeed.

## Warning

> [!WARNING]
> Something that needs attention.

## Caution

> [!CAUTION]
> A risk such as losing data.

## Lowercase marker

> [!note]
> A marker in lowercase still works.

## Rich callout

> [!NOTE]
> A callout with several kinds of content.
>
> - A list item
> - Another list item
>
> ```bash
> echo "code inside a callout"
> ```

## Plain quote

> A blockquote without a marker stays a plain quote.
